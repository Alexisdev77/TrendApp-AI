## Who is it for?

For you if you want to launch a mobile app that earns money, without knowing where to start:
- you don't know which idea to pick;
- you can't code, or only a little;
- you only have a few hours a week;
- you want to know how to find your first users.

## How it works

1. **You answer a free questionnaire**: your level, your available time, your income goal, what is holding you back today.
2. **You discover your 3 app ideas**, picked for your profile, each with a compatibility score, the reason it was chosen and one thing to watch out for.
3. **You choose your idea** and see what its App Store page could look like.
4. **TrendApp AI prepares your project**:
   - the build prompt, to paste into Claude;
   - depending on your plan, your build plan and your marketing strategy.
5. **You build your app with Claude**, step by step, then you launch it.

## Full transparency

- **The ideas come from a calculation.** TrendApp AI compares your answers with a catalogue of app ideas written and rated by the team: complexity, speed to launch, revenue potential, competition. These are editorial estimates, not market research.
- **TrendApp AI does not write your code.** It prepares a detailed prompt that you paste into Claude, which helps you build your app. You follow the steps and test the result.
- **No income is guaranteed.** Results depend on your product, your execution and the market.

## FAQ

**Can I build an app without knowing how to code?**
Yes, with Claude and the build prompt prepared for your idea. Nothing is automatic: you move forward step by step.

**How long does it take?**
The questionnaire takes a few minutes. Building depends on your idea and your available time: the build plan (Builder and Founder) splits the work over 90 days.

**What if I don't like any of the 3 ideas?**
Your 3 ideas stay assigned to you. You can switch between them at any time, and adapt your idea with change requests.
